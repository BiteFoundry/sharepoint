## What's Changed

### Modular Architecture & Terminal Safety
- **Terminal & Agent Safe-Output (`src/safe_text.rs`)**: Sanitized untrusted SharePoint site, drive, file, folder, list, and search metadata rendered to CLI terminal output against ANSI/VT100 escape sequences (CSI/OSC/DCS/C1), C0/C1 control characters, Unicode Bidi overrides, and invisible Unicode Tag characters (`U+E0000..=U+E007F`), while preserving raw `--json` output.
- **Supply Chain & Packaging**: Added `[package.metadata.binstall]` for `cargo-binstall`, `deny.toml` + `.github/workflows/audit.yml` (`cargo-deny` CI), and `refs/tags/v` tag guards on publish/release jobs.
- **Codebase Modularization**: Decomposed `src/cli.rs`, `src/ui/app.rs` (`apply_ui_msg` 487L → 147L), and `tests/mock_graph_e2e.rs` into focused domain submodules.
