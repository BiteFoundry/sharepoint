## What's Changed

### Performance Optimization Suite (Async Offloading, TUI Preview Cache & Pixel Pipeline)
- **Async Runtime & SQLite**: Offloaded blocking SQLite reads/writes in `serve.rs`, `sync.rs`, and `ui/app/requests.rs` (plus image decoding) onto `tokio::task::spawn_blocking` and converted the last uncached `prepare` call to `prepare_cached`.
- **Zero-Clone TUI Preview & Deferred Pixels**: Replaced per-frame FNV-1a preview hashing with `text_preview_gen`, pre-wrapped inspector lines once per `(gen, width)` with visible-window slicing, eliminated 2-pass `html_preview` cache thrashing, sliced visible rows in overlays, and deferred `viewport_pixel_payload` until `plan_transition` emits.
- **Pixel Engine**: Upgraded Sixel quantization to flat 32K histogram + 5-5-5 LUT + 2-row `i16` dithering + fast ASCII RLE, switched PNG encoding to `Fast`/`NoFilter` (`fdeflate`), and enabled `opt-level = 3` for codec/pixel crates.
